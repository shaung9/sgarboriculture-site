City of Monash

Tree controls in Monash

Monash is straightforward, because it decided against having a tree local law at all. A March 2024 council report concluded that a local law for canopy tree removal was not recommended, so the planning scheme is the whole of it.

Glen Waverley, Mount Waverley, Clayton, Oakleigh, Chadstone, Mulgrave, Notting Hill, Ashwood

At a glance

Local law
None for private trees. Council specifically decided against one in March 2024
Vegetation Protection Overlay (VPO) trigger
Vegetation higher than 10 m AND trunk circumference greater than 50 cm, measured at 120 cm above ground level
Clause 52.37
Applies as per the state provision. Over 5 m, circumference over 0.5 m, canopy 4 m or more
Where 52.37 bites
Anywhere on the site if vacant, otherwise within 6 m of the narrowest street frontage or 4.5 m of the rear boundary
Significant Landscape Overlay
Applies along Gardiners Creek. The schedule works by exemption: trees under the stated dimensions, and declared weed species, fall outside it
52.37 exemptions Council publishes
Dead trees, immediate risk of injury or property damage, and pruning of no more than one third of foliage
VPO application fees
The fee exemption for VPO tree removal applications was discontinued from 1 July 2024
Bonds
No tree bond. A landscape bond was flagged for investigation in March 2024
Canopy target
30% across Monash by 2040

Drawn from Council's published material as at August 2026. Councils update without notice, so confirm anything you are relying on for a lodgement.

The overlay measures lower down the trunk than Clause 52.37 does

The Monash VPO measures circumference at 120 cm above ground level. Clause 52.37 and AS 4970:2025 both measure at 140 cm. On a tree with any flare at the base those two heights give you different figures, and a tree can sit over the threshold on one and under it on the other. Merri-bek measures at 120 cm as well, so if you work across both councils the habit is at least consistent.

The VPO test is also conjunctive. A tree must be over 10 m tall and over 50 cm circumference at 1.2 m. Miss either limb and the VPO does not reach the tree, though Clause 52.37 still might.

The free advice service is worth using

Monash offers a free service for residents unsure whether a permit is required in a VPO area. For a straightforward single tree question that is the fastest answer available, and there is no reason to pay anybody for it.

What Monash does not publish

For removal on health or stability grounds Monash asks only for an assessment by "an arborist or other qualified person". No checklist, no minimum qualification, no mention of AS 4970. A report that states its method and works to the current standard clears that bar comfortably, but you get no credit for matching a Council template because there is not one.

A long running amendment worth checking

Monash lodged Amendment C165 with the Minister on 15 October 2019 seeking municipality wide tree controls. It was still undecided as at the 2024 report. Clause 52.37 has arrived in the meantime and may well have overtaken it, as happened to Whitehorse's SLO9. If you are planning around today's position on a long lead time project, check where C165 has landed.
