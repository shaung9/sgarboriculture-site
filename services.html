Services

Arborist services in Melbourne, from site acquisition to sign-off

This is a consulting practice only. I do not prune, remove or climb, so nothing I recommend earns me any work on the other side of it. Every service is quoted as a fixed fee, agreed in writing before work begins.

Services

Tree Removal Report

Council has asked for an arborist report before it will consider removing your tree. This is that report, and it is usually the whole job.

Residential

Arboricultural Impact Assessment

What councils ask for when your works reach into a tree's root zone. Each tree assessed, the zones calculated to AS 4970:2025, and the encroachment measured and justified.

Development

Clause 52.37 Canopy Tree Report

The provision that arrived in September 2025 and now catches trees nothing else reached. Which of yours are caught, whether you need a permit, and what replacement planting you owe.

Residential · Planning

Preliminary Tree Assessment

Commissioned before the design is drawn. Tells you what is on the site, which trees are protected, and where the root zones are going to sit against your footprint.

Before you design

Tree Protection Management Plan

The TPMP that gets conditioned on the permit, written to AS 4970:2025. Specific enough that a planner can enforce it and a site foreman can work to it.

Development

Root & Infrastructure Investigation

Finding out what the roots are actually doing without cutting them, and whether the tree is really behind the problem. Sewers, footings, paving and retaining walls.

Investigation

Arboricultural Supervision

Named on the permit, on site at the hold points and signing off each stage. AS 4970:2025 assumes an arborist stays involved for the length of the project.

Construction Monitoring

Booked attendance across the build, with the stage records council needs to close out the tree conditions on your permit.

Which one do you need, and when

Under AS 4970:2025 the reporting is staged. Most projects need two or three of these, in this order.

Before design: preliminary tree assessment

Which trees are on the site, which are protected, and where the root zones will sit against your footprint. Of everything here this is the one that saves people the most money.

With the application: impact assessment, plus Clause 52.37 if triggered

The permit documents. What the works do to each tree, and whether canopy trees are caught.

At endorsement, when council signs off the permit drawings: tree protection plan and specifications

How the retained trees are protected. Usually a condition of the permit.

During construction: project arborist attendance

Hold points, supervised excavation, stage sign-offs, and the documentation that closes the conditions out.
