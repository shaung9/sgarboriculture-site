Standards

AS 4970:2025: what changed, and what your council now asks for

The Notional Root Zone replaced the calculated Tree Protection Zone, Diameter at Breast Height became Diameter at Standard Height, and encroachment is now measured against the area of that zone rather than its radius. Most of the Melbourne market has not caught up, and two firms still advertise the 2009 edition.

Why these exist

Two provisions landed in 2025 and changed how tree work is assessed in Victoria. AS 4970 was revised in May, and Clause 52.37 arrived in September. Between them they altered the vocabulary of every impact assessment and created a permit trigger that reaches trees nothing else reached.

A year on, most of the published guidance still describes the old position. Councils cite the superseded standard in their own documents. The reference body many of them defer to still publishes a calculator built on the 2009 formulas. Two Melbourne consultancies still advertise the 2009 edition as the standard they work to.

That is not really anyone's fault. Councils and reference bodies take time to update their documents. It does mean that someone trying to do the right thing can read three sources and come away with three answers, so these pages set out what the provisions say and flag where the published summaries have it wrong.
